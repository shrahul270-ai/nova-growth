Real-time glass, in five terms.

Iridescence — Thin-film optical wave interference producing structural colour and iridescent lustre as viewing angle changes relative to incident illumination.

Index of refraction — Physical ratio governing phase velocity of light within the dielectric medium, dictating angular boundary bending and Fresnel reflectance.

Dispersion — Variation of refractive index with spectral wavelength, resolving collimated white beams into chromatic Abbe-number fringes at prismatic shard facets.

Transmission — Volumetric ratio of specular photon throughput through non-scattering glass interior, free from internal absorption.

Roughness — Statistical microfacet slope distribution controlling micro-scattering, softening specular reflection lobes and transmitted highlights.
